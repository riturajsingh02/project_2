Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Makhanam (A Brand of Kaushik Global Exports), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal and corporate information you share with us during B2B inquiries, wholesale orders, and website browsing.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an RFQ (Request for Quote), WhatsApp enquiry, or contact form on our website, we may collect:

  • Full name, corporate name, and job title.
  • Contact details including email address and phone/WhatsApp number.
  • Procurement requirements, preferred Suta grades (3+ to 7+ Suta), and destination port details.
  • Technical communication records and correspondence logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is strictly utilized for:

  • Processing and responding to your wholesale inquiries and sample requests.
  • Generating commercial invoices, freight dispatches, and export compliance documentation.
  • Communicating updates regarding shipments, FSSAI quality certificates, and GI-tag verification logs.
  • Improving our website user experience and secure communication channels.

3. Information Security

We implement rigorous administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your proprietary corporate data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Your data is never sold, leased, or rented to third-party marketing entities.
