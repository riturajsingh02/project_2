B2B Quality Assurance Guarantee

Because we operate as a direct processor and exporter of GI-tagged Mithila makhana with rigorous caliper sorting (3 to 7+ Suta) and moisture control below 10%, we stand firmly behind the superior quality of every dispatch batch.

1. Pre-Dispatch Sample Verification

To eliminate discrepancies in commercial bulk shipments, we strongly encourage all B2B wholesale buyers to request sample packets prior to finalizing container or tonnage dispatches. Our physical samples accurately reflect the exact Suta grade, crispness, and moisture levels of your final bulk order.

2. Claims & Inspection Protocol

In the rare event of transit damage or grade discrepancy upon arrival at your designated warehouse or port:

  • Claims must be lodged in writing within 7 business days of delivery.
  • Detailed photographic/video evidence of container seals, packaging sacks, and batch inspection reports must be provided.
  • All products must remain in their original hermetically sealed food-grade packaging.

3. Resolution & Replacements

Upon joint verification by our quality control desk in Purnia, validated claims will be promptly resolved through replacement shipments in subsequent dispatches or proportional credit adjustments against future purchase orders.
