SEQUENTIAL ARTISANAL ROADMAP

The Preparation of Makhana.

From muddy pond beds to pure white puffed blooms — follow the step-by-step roadmap of how authentic Bihar Makhana is crafted.

PHASE 01 · GATHERING

Pond Bed Harvesting

STEP 01 · RAW GURI

Mallah divers forage muddy wetland beds by hand to collect fresh black seeds (*Guri*) without cracking the outer shell.

Traditional Diving 01 / 06
PHASE 02 · DRYING

Sun-Drying & Conditioning

STEP 02 · SUN CURED

Freshly washed seeds are spread across open bamboo mats under natural sunshine to stabilize moisture down to 31–33%.

2–3 Days Sunning 02 / 06
PHASE 03 · GRADING

Pre-Roast Size Sorting

STEP 03 · JHARNI SIEVE

Dry seeds are sieved through multi-mesh iron sieves (*Jharni*) into 5 uniform sizes to guarantee identical heat absorption.

5 Size Calibers 03 / 06
PHASE 04 · ROASTING

Wood-Fired Pan Roasting

STEP 04 · 250°C–300°C

Constant high-heat stirring in cast-iron pans loosens the delicate inner white kernel from the rock-hard exterior coat.

High Thermal Shock 04 / 06
PHASE 05 · EXPANSION

Wooden Mallet Popping

STEP 05 · THAPI POPPING

A swift, precise manual strike with a hardwood mallet (*Thapi*) instantly pops the red-hot kernel into a crisp white puff.

Artisanal Precision 05 / 06
PHASE 06 · PACKAGING

Caliper Sorting & Pack

STEP 06 · EXPORT READY

Popped grains are calibrated into 3 to 7 Suta grades, dedusted, and hermetically sealed in moisture-barrier master packaging.

Moisture Barrier 06 / 06

GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION (GI TAG)

Certified Mithila Makhana

Mithila Makhana holds official Government of India Geographical Indication (GI) certification, legally recognizing Northern Bihar's unique aquatic terroir, generational seed craftsmanship, and pristine nutritional density that cannot be replicated anywhere else in the world.

01. Legal Terroir Protection

Officially registered under the Geographical Indications of Goods Act, protecting authentic cultivation across the perennial oxbow wetlands and river basins of Mithila.

02. Artisanal Harvest Integrity

Mandates traditional, chemical-free processing: underwater foraging, natural sun-curing, and clay-roast mallet expansion that preserves pure botanical nutrients.

03. Global Authenticity Guarantee

Protects international food brands and bulk buyers against counterfeit adulteration, ensuring every consignment is 100% verifiable single-origin Bihar Makhana.
