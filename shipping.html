Pan-India & Global Export Logistics

Operating from our central grading and processing facilities in Purnia, Bihar, we coordinate seamless freight dispatches via national highways (NH-31 & NH-27) and execute secure FOB/CIF export consignments worldwide.

1. Processing & Dispatch Timelines

Standard wholesale orders are hermetically packed in food-grade multi-layer bags or vacuum cartons and dispatched within 3 to 7 business days following order confirmation and quality clearance.

2. Domestic & International Transit

  • Domestic Dispatches: Covered via verified road transport freight partners across major Indian hubs with typical transit windows of 3 to 7 days.
  • Global Exports: Managed via air and container freight corridors (FOB/CIF terms) with complete phytosanitary certificates, FSSAI export clearance, and GI-tag documentation.
